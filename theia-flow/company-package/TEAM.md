# Theia Flow Intelligence Command

This package establishes the initial command organization for Theia Flow.

The CIO is the organization root. The CIOA coordinates division chiefs and monitors cross-division execution. The Chief of Staff supports the CIO with prioritization, briefing preparation, and organizational follow-through. QA remains directly accountable to the CIO to preserve independent quality oversight.

Division chiefs own Requirements, Collection, Processing, Analysis, Dissemination, and Operations/Effects.

PaperClip provides the organization, delegation, execution, skills, workspaces, connections, approvals, recovery, and audit substrate. Intelligence doctrine and mission semantics remain in the Theia Flow intelligence layer.
